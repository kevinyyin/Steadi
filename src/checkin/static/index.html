    Before you start

    • The belt is around the lower back and switched on.
    • An arm chair, with a line on the floor 3 metres (10 feet) in front of it.
    • A chair without arms, against a wall.
    • A kitchen counter or sturdy table to stand beside.
    • Someone stays beside you the whole time.

      For the doctor

      Fall-risk screening with the CDC's STEADI tests, run at home by the family and scored by a lower-back motion sensor: it flags increased fall risk and tracks change from baseline. It does not diagnose. The balance test stops at the tandem stance (no single-leg stand), and the rest of a STEADI assessment (medications, postural blood pressure, vision, feet and footwear, home hazards) isn't covered.
